What gets logged

Every change made in the dashboard or through the API: domains, mailboxes, aliases, team members, invitations, API keys and billing. Each event shows who made it and when.

Who sees what

  • Owners and admins see everything.
  • Billing sees only billing events.
  • Helpdesk and read only see domain and mailbox events.
