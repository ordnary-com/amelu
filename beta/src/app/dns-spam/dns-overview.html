Adding records by hand

  • Copy each field into the matching field at your provider. Leave the TTL at its default.
  • Already have an MX or SPF record from another mail provider? Replace it, don't add a second one.
  • Changes can take a few minutes to an hour to show up. Use Check again after saving.
