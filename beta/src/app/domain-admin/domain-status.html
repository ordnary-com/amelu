Why we pause sending

Every Amelu customer sends from the same mail servers. When one domain sends mail people didn't ask for, providers like Gmail and Outlook start refusing mail from all of us. Pausing that domain early keeps delivery working for everyone, including you.

Cold outreach, purchased or scraped lists and mail to people who never signed up aren't allowed, whatever the volume. Acceptable Use Policy
