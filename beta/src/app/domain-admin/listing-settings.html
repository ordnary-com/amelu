What listing does

A listed domain may be shown by name in a public directory of domains hosted on Amelu. It doesn't expose any mailboxes, addresses or mail.

Off by default

New domains are never listed until you turn this on. Mail delivery works the same either way.

Per mailbox

Mailboxes have their own listing setting, under each mailbox in Mailboxes.
