New owner

The email address they sign in to Amelu with. They need an account already.

What moves

The domain with all of its mailboxes, messages, aliases and settings. Mail keeps flowing during and after the transfer; only who manages it changes.

Before you transfer

  • Only the organization owner can transfer a domain.
  • The new owner needs an existing Amelu account.
  • You can't undo it yourself; the new owner would have to transfer it back.

Just want mail to stop?

Deactivate the domain instead. It pauses delivery and keeps everything in your account.
