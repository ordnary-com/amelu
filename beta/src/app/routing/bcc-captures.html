How captures work

  • Applies to mail delivered to any mailbox on this domain.
  • The original is delivered as normal; the copy goes out on the side.
  • The copy address can be on any domain, for example an archive.

Mind the privacy rules

Copying someone's mail without them knowing can be restricted where you or they live. Let mailbox users know when their mail is archived.
