How it works

With example.org as an alias of {{ ctx.domain()?.name || 'this domain' }}, mail to sales@example.org is delivered as if it was sent to sales@{{ ctx.domain()?.name || 'this domain' }}. Mailboxes and aliases are only managed here, on the main domain.

DNS for the alias domain

Mail only arrives once the alias domain's MX records point at the same mail servers as this domain. Set them at your DNS provider for the alias domain.
