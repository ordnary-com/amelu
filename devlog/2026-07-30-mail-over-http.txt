Read and send mail over HTTP, so agents don't need IMAP
30 July 2026

Someone in the showcase channel asked whether Amelu could give AI agents their
own inboxes. Most of that already existed - a mailbox per agent, provisioned
over the API, with its own credentials - the gap was that reading and sending
meant speaking IMAP and SMTP.

Three endpoints, all API-key friendly:
GET  /api/mailboxes/{id}/messages?folder=inbox|sent&limit=&position=
GET  /api/mailboxes/{id}/messages/{messageId}
POST /api/mailboxes/{id}/messages

They sit on JMAP, extending what ListRecentEmails already did. Reading is
Email/query then Email/get with fetchTextBodyValues; bodies are capped at
256KB and multi-part bodies are joined in order rather than taking part one.
Sending is Email/set into Drafts then EmailSubmission/set, two requests rather
than one with #back-references, so a failed submission is distinguishable from
a failed build - and the orphaned draft gets destroyed instead of sitting in
the mailbox.

There is no from field on send. The From and the SMTP envelope are both the
submitting mailbox, so a key cannot send as a colleague. Recipients are
rejected if they contain a newline, comma or space, which is the header
injection case: "a@example.com\nBcc: victim@example.com" is a 400, not a
silent extra recipient.

New authz.CanAccessMailboxContents, owner and admin only - deliberately
narrower than CanManageMailboxes. Helpdesk resetting a password is a visible
support action; helpdesk silently reading someone's mail is not.

Not verified against a live Stalwart - marduk isn't reachable from this
machine, /jmap returns 401 with the local .env. Covered instead by a fake JMAP
server in messages_test.go that pins the request shapes. The submission
envelope and onSuccessUpdateEmail move are the two bits most likely to need a
correction once tried for real.

Still missing, and the thing that was actually asked for: a webhook on
inbound mail. Polling works but is not what an agent wants.
