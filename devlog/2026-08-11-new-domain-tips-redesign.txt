New Domain's floating tips card replaced with plain copy
11 August 2026

New Domain had a sticky sidebar TipsCard (carousel, dots, Tip 1 of 3,
Back/Next buttons) next to the form. All three tips just restated content
already written into the form itself - "skip the www" duplicated the intro
paragraph, "DNS changes are required" duplicated the DNS Nameservers
section, "default addresses save a step" duplicated the Default Email
Addresses section. So the card wasn't adding information, just the same
copy twice in two different widgets.

Redesigned to match Import Mailboxes' style instead: single column,
.import-form class, plain paragraphs in the normal flow, no card, no
carousel state. Removed the page-local TipsCard function and TIPS array,
and the now-unused .new-domain-layout/.new-domain-form rules in app.css.

Left alone: components/TipsCard.tsx and the shared .tips-card CSS - that's
a separate, reusable component that was never actually wired into
NewDomainPage (it had its own local duplicate instead), unrelated to this
change.
