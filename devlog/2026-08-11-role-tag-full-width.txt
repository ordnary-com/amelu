Your Role badge on My Organization stretched full-width
11 August 2026

Screenshot showed the OWNER badge on My Organization as a full-width green
bar instead of a compact pill. .kv-row (app.css) is display: grid with a
1fr value column and no justify-items override, so its default stretch
applies to whatever sits directly in that column. Everywhere else .tag
badges are wrapped in a plain <span> or sit outside a grid, so the stretch
never showed up - MyOrganizationPage.tsx:150 puts the role <span
className={ROLE_TAG_CLASS[...]}> straight in as the second grid child,
so it inherited the full column width. .tag's own display: inline-flex
only governs its children's layout, not whether the grid stretches its
outer box.

Added justify-self: start to .tag in app.css instead of wrapping the one
call site - fixes it at the source for any future direct-grid-child usage
too, and is a no-op outside grid containers.
