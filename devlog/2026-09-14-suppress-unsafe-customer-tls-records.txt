Customer HTTPS discovery records hidden until TLS supports them
14 September 2026

Stalwart generated mta-sts, autoconfig, autodiscover, and ua-auto-config
CNAMEs for every customer domain, all pointing at marduk.mx.amelu.org.
HTTPS requests kept the customer hostname in SNI, while marduk served a
certificate covering only marduk, so hostname verification failed.
FilterTLSIncompatibleRecords now removes those CNAMEs and the related
_mta-sts and _ua-auto-config TXT advertisements from the DNS API and BIND
downloads without parsing and rewriting the 2048-bit RSA DKIM record.
Domain Connect no longer includes the same records or variables.
MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, TLS-RPT, and service SRV records are unchanged.
The filter stays until per-customer ACME or a multi-tenant HTTPS endpoint
can present a valid certificate for every advertised customer hostname.
