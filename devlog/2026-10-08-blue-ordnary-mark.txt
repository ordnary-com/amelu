Login button uses the blue Ordnary mark
2026-10-08

The Login with Ordnary account button on /login showed the old Ordnary
icon, forced to white with a CSS filter. It now shows the blue dot-ring
mark from Ordnary ID (frontend/public/ordnary-mark-blue.png), without the
filter, so it keeps its brand blue on the dark button.
The old frontend/public/ordnary-icon.png was no longer used and is removed.
