Login shows progress while redirecting to Ordnary
2026-10-08

Clicking Login with Ordnary account left /login unchanged for several
seconds while the browser went to api.amelu.org and on to Ordnary ID,
longer when the API container had to start. It looked like nothing happened.
The click now runs a thin blue progress bar along the top of the page and
swaps the Ordnary mark in the button for a spinner; a second click does
nothing. A first version covered the whole page with an overlay and text,
which felt heavier than it needed to. Coming back with the back button
resets the button (pageshow from the back/forward cache).
/login also ignored ?error= from the callback. ordnary_auth_failed and
ordnary_email_unverified now show a short message above the button.
