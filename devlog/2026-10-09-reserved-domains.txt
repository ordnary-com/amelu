Ordnary's own domains can no longer be added as customer domains
2026-10-09

A customer had added ordnary.com as a domain on 2026-07-24. Stalwart
delivers mail for any domain it hosts locally instead of following MX,
so once it was active every Amelu sender writing to an ordnary.com
address got "Mailbox does not exist" from marduk. The domain, its
admin/postmaster/abuse accounts and DKIM signatures were removed from
Stalwart by hand.
POST /api/domains and POST /api/domains/{id}/aliases now refuse
ordnary.com, ordnary.cloud, amelu.org and any subdomain of them with
403 "this domain is reserved and can't be added" (isReservedDomain in
handlers/reserved_domains.go).
