Email and Password removed from My Account, sign-in is Ordnary-only
2026-10-11

My Account still had Email and Password pages from the old local login.
Sign-in only goes through the Ordnary account center now, so neither did
anything useful: Ordnary-provisioned accounts get a random, unusable
password hash, so "Current password" could never match and both forms
always failed with "current password is incorrect".
Removed the /account/email and /account/password pages and their sidebar
links, and the PATCH /api/account/email and PATCH /api/account/password
endpoints (UpdateAccountEmail, UpdateAccountPassword, and the store's
UpdateCustomerEmail and UpdateCustomerPassword). The Overview still shows
the sign-in email, now read-only with "Managed by Ordnary" instead of a
Manage link.
