Loading screen before the dashboard looked broken
2026-10-11

While the session check (GET /api/account) was in flight, ProtectedRoute
rendered a bare "Loading..." paragraph. With no layout around it, the text
ended up top-left on an empty page background, and then the whole header
and sidebar popped in at once, which read as a glitch.
It now renders .app-loading (app.css): a full-screen panel in the same
--chrome-bg blue as the header and sidebar, with the sliding 3px bar the
login page already uses. The layout swaps in without a color flash. The
bar slows down under prefers-reduced-motion.
Bumped index.html's link to /app.css?v=3. The live response for app.css
still carries Cache-Control max-age=14400 despite the must-revalidate
rule in public/_headers, so without the bump returning visitors keep the
old stylesheet for up to four hours. That is also why the Your Role badge
fix looked like it hadn't shipped.
