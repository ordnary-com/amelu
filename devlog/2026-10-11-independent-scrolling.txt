a big address list) the sidebar slid up and out of view with the content.

flex: none and height: 100vh (flex: 1 alone let the flex basis win over the
height), main gets min-height: 0, and .container and .sidemenu-container
each get overflow-y: auto. The sidebar also needed flex: none, or main
squeezed it below its menus' width once it had an overflow and labels like
"Usage Instructions" got cut off. It has a thin translucent scrollbar so it
doesn't sit on top of the third menu.
Layout scrolls .container back to the top on every route change; the window
used to be the scroller, now a new page would open at the old one's offset.
Login, invite and reset pages don't render <main> and still scroll normally.
