2.0: My Account
2026-10-12

My Account in the beta dashboard, with Overview, Appearance and API Keys
tabs. There is no General tab any more: name, photo, email and two-step
verification are managed in the Ordnary account, so the overview shows them
read-only with a link there, plus role and last sign-in. The username isn't
shown; it's only used internally.
Appearance has Dark, Light and System tiles with a small preview. API Keys
lists keys with prefix, created and last used, creates one and shows the raw
key once with Copy, and asks before revoking. The terminate card links to
/account/terminate.
