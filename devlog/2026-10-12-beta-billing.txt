2.0: Billing
2026-10-12

Billing in the beta dashboard with Overview, Plans and Invoices tabs.
Overview: plan, price line, subscription status, payment method, the last
three invoices, domain usage, and Manage subscription for the Stripe billing
portal. The ?checkout=success/cancelled return shows a green or yellow
alert. A free plan's price line says "No charge, no card needed" instead of
repeating "Free" under the plan name.
Plans: Free, Go and Pro with a Monthly/Yearly toggle and the yearly saving,
Switch to (Stripe checkout) or Cancel plan, and the same feature lists as
the current Plans page. Invoices: table with View and PDF links.
Stripe still sends people back to the current dashboard after checkout or
the portal; those return URLs are set in the backend.
