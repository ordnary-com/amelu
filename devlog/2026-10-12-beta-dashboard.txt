2.0: dashboard home
2026-10-12

The home page of the beta dashboard: greeting hero with Changelog and
Support links (icon-only on phones), the six quick actions of the current
dashboard with its 26px icons, one card per email domain with its status
badge and MX/SPF/DKIM chips plus a dashed card to add one, and a side
column with the plan (domain usage bar, red when over the limit, Upgrade)
and the latest invoice.
The DNS chips come from GET /api/domains/{id}/dns per domain, checked once
and shared with other pages (DomainHealthChecks). DKIM shows grey "not
checked" rather than red, because that endpoint returns no _domainkey
records yet and every domain would look broken.
