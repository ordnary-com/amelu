2.0: DNS setup and spam filtering
2026-10-12

/domains/{id}/dns shows how many records are verified, the MX/SPF/DKIM
summary and only the records still to add or fix; /dns/records lists every
record with a filter. Each record has copy buttons for name and value, its
live status, and what is actually published when it differs. Check again
refetches GET /api/domains/{id}/dns and reloads the domain list when the
result flips the domain between active and DNS pending. Automatic setup
(Domain Connect) and the zone file download stay.
Names are shown as the short host (@, _dmarc) providers ask for. The MX
priority is shown as 10, hard-coded: the check response doesn't include it.
Spam: overview, subject settings, sender lists and recipient denylist, with
wildcards in the denylist caught before saving.
