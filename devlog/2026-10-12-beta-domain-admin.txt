2.0: Domain activity, transfer, deactivate, delete, listing and notes
2026-10-12

The remaining domain pages in the beta dashboard: recent activity grouped
by day with filters (admin.* events show "By Amelu support"), transfer
ownership, deactivate or reactivate, delete, listing settings and attached
notes.
Delete now asks you to type the domain name before it goes ahead; the old
page only had a button. After transfer, deactivate or delete the domain
list reloads so the sidebar and badges follow. The notes text said "visible
only to you" while domains belong to the organization; it now says so.
There is no single-domain endpoint, so listing and notes read their values
from GET /api/domains.
