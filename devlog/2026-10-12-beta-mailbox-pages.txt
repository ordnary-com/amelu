2.0: Mailbox pages
2026-10-12

Every page of a single mailbox, at the old paths under
/domains/{id}/mailboxes/{mailboxId}: overview, activity, logs, usage
instructions (with copy buttons), enabled services, limits, password,
internal access, identities, forwarding, delegation, expiration, listing
settings, notes and delete. A shared layout loads GET /api/mailboxes/{id}
once and keeps the header (address, status, display name) in step after
changes.
The old overview hard-coded "Internal access only", the IMAP/POP3/Sieve
rows and "Expires: Never"; it now shows the real values with a Change link,
and the attached notes it promised.
