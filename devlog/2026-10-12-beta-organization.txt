2.0: My Organization and Activity Log
2026-10-12

My Organization lists members with a role select and Remove (with a
confirmation), pending and expired invitations with Revoke, the
organization's details, and an invite form. When no email delivery is set
up the API returns the invite link, which is shown to copy instead.
The Activity Log groups GET /api/organization/audit by local day (it was
grouped by UTC day at first, putting late-evening events on the next day),
filters by Team, Domains, Mailboxes, Billing and Account, loads older pages
with Load more, and explains who sees what. That text follows
authz.VisibleAuditObjectTypes: billing members only see billing events,
helpdesk and read only see domain and mailbox events.
