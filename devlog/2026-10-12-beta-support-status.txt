2.0: Support, Changelog, Status and Terminate account
2026-10-12

The last pages of the beta dashboard outside a domain. Support keeps the old
wording and contact link and adds what to include in a request. Changelog
and Status are still static data in the page, as in the current dashboard;
Status shows every service as operational by hand and needs a real health
check before anyone relies on it.
Terminate account calls DELETE /api/account with the current password and
signs out, as before, behind a confirmation. Known problem: accounts that
only ever signed in through Ordnary have no usable Amelu password, so they
can't terminate here. That needs a backend change.
