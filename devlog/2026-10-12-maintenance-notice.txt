Maintenance notice on app.amelu.org
2026-10-12

A notice now says Amelu is undergoing maintenance and that some features
may be temporarily unavailable. In the dashboard it is a thin red strip with just the text across the top
of the content of every page (light and dark variants); on the login page
an amber box above the login button, so it is seen before signing in too.
The first version put a red bar under the header
styled from app.css, which showed up unstyled: app.css is not content-hashed
and was still cached from before the deploy (see 2026-07-31). The banner now
has its own CSS module (MaintenanceBanner.module.css), bundled and hashed.
A big red card with a title and icon in between was too loud for the
dashboard and was toned down to the strip.
The text lives in one place, MAINTENANCE_NOTICE in frontend/src/maintenance.ts.
Setting it to null hides both and needs a frontend redeploy (manual
wrangler pages deploy, see docs/FRONTEND_DEPLOYS.md).
