2.0: new 404 page
2026-10-13

The not-found page in the 2.0 dashboard was a small card. It is now plain
content on the page: a large 404, the path that doesn't exist, "Go to the
dashboard" and "Go back" (history back), links to Email domains, My account
and Service status, and a link to Support. The login page is now lazy
loaded, which keeps the initial bundle under the 500 kB budget.
