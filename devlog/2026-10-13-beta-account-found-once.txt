2.0: the account confirmation only right after signing in
2026-10-13

"We found your Amelu account. Continue with it?" came back in every new
browser tab, and after a tab was restored, because the confirmation was
kept per tab in sessionStorage (amelu.account-confirmed) and the auth guard
demanded it on every page. With a valid session the dashboard now just
opens, in every tab, until the session expires.

The step still shows once after signing in: the login page sets
amelu.login-pending in sessionStorage before leaving for Ordnary (sign in
and Use another account), the guard sends a tab with that flag to /login
after the callback, and Yes, continue clears it. Signing out clears it too.
