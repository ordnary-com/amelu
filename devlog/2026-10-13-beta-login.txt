2.0: own sign-in page, login returns to the dashboard that started it
2026-10-13

The 2.0 dashboard sent signed-out visitors to the old dashboard's /login.
It now has its own /login page (beta/src/app/login) with the same "Sign in
with Ordnary" button and error messages; the auth guard sends you there with
returnTo set, and logout lands there too. Next to the card is a carousel of
five Unsplash photos (beta/public/login, credits in CREDITS.txt) that
changes every 6 seconds, and stays put with prefers-reduced-motion.

CORS_ORIGIN is now a comma separated list (first entry stays the primary for
emailed links and Stripe redirects). The CORS middleware echoes the request
Origin only when it is on that list. GET /api/auth/ordnary/login takes an
optional origin parameter, checked against the list and kept in the signed
login-state cookie; the callback redirects back to that origin, errors
included. returnTo must now be a path: "//evil.example" or "@evil.example"
fall back to /domains instead of leaving the site.

The legal line links to amelu.org/terms, /acceptable-use and /privacy. At
the bottom of the page: version and commit (scripts/build-info.mjs writes
src/app/build-info.ts before every start, build and test), build time, the
edge location from /cdn-cgi/trace (e.g. AMS, NL, HTTP/2, TLS 1.3) and the
round trip to GET /api/healthz.
