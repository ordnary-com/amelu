Repair mailboxes the mail cluster lost
2026-10-13

Mailboxes from before the Stalwart data loss of 2026-08-23 exist in Amelu's
database but not in the cluster, so every cluster-backed page failed with
MAIL_CLUSTER_NOT_FOUND ("could not load incoming messages: object not found
in mail cluster"). POST /api/mailboxes/{id}/repair recreates the domain in
Stalwart when it is missing (new DKIM key, status back to dns_pending) and
the account with a fresh password, then re-applies the domain defaults and
spam/capture rules, like creation does. POST
/api/domains/{id}/mailboxes/repair does the same for every mailbox of a
domain and lists the recreated ones with their passwords.

2.0 dashboard: perMailbox now records the failed load's message and code
(load-errors.ts) and the error empty-states print the message. A mailbox
page whose load failed with MAIL_CLUSTER_NOT_FOUND shows a warning with
"Repair mailbox"; the domain's Mailboxes page has "Repair on mail servers".
Old mail is not recovered by any of this.
