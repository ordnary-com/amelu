Avatars on team members and in the activity log
2026-10-13

GET /api/organization/members now returns avatarUrl per member and the
organization audit list returns actorAvatarUrl per entry, both from
customers.avatar_url (the Ordnary picture, migration 0029). The audit query
LEFT JOINs customers on actor_customer_id, so entries from deleted members
still come back, just without a picture. Both fields are omitted when empty.

In the 2.0 dashboard a shared amelu-avatar component (beta/src/app/avatar.ts)
shows the picture, or initials when there is none or it fails to load. My
Organization uses it in the member list; the Activity Log shows a small one
in front of the actor's email.
