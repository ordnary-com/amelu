2.0: Illustrations in empty states
2026-10-23

Every empty list and failed load in the 2.0 dashboard was a bold title and
a line of grey text in a big white card. amelu-empty-state now takes an
illustration input, and 25 of them have one: an empty mailbox for "No
mailboxes yet", a magnifier for every "No matching ..." search, an invoice
for "No invoices yet", and so on. Every failed load (error set) gets the
server-down one automatically. Compact side cards stay text only.

The 15 SVGs come from undraw.co and live in public/illustrations. Their
purple, greys and near-black were rewritten to --ill-accent, --ill-white,
--ill-light, --ill-mid and --ill-dark (styles.css), and the component
inlines the SVG instead of using an img, so they follow light and dark mode.
Each file is fetched once and cached. One clipPath id was "a" and is now
ill-authentication-a, since several inline SVGs can share a page.
