2.0: Beta notice moved into the greeting card
2026-10-23

The beta notice was its own card under the greeting ("You're using the new
Amelu dashboard, which is still being built."), with a circleAlert icon, a
rounded-full Beta pill that sat a pixel or two off the text, and a close
button. It read like an error and took a whole row on the home page.

It now sits at the top of the greeting card itself, left of the Changelog,
Support and Discord links. Those links used to be absolutely positioned;
notice and links now share one flex row, so they can't overlap at any
width. The Beta badge is an 18px box with 3px corners, aligned to the first
line. No icon, no close button: the amelu.beta-banner.dismissed flag and
the beta-banner component are gone, and GITHUB_URL moved to config.ts.
