Unlimited storage on every plan
2026-10-23

The plan lists said "25 GB storage per mailbox" on Go and "100 GB storage
per mailbox" on Pro, and nothing on Free. None of it was enforced:
CreateMailbox sends Stalwart no quota, max_disk_quota_bytes defaults to 0
and is then not sent at all, so every mailbox on every plan had no storage
limit. A customer asking how much storage Free includes is what showed it.

All three plans now say "Unlimited storage per mailbox", in both dashboards
(BillingPlansPage.tsx and billing-plans.ts) and on amelu.org. The Terms of
Service still list storage among the plan limits; that's legal text and
hasn't been changed yet.
