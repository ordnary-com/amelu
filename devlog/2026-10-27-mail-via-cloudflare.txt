Mail through Cloudflare Email Sending instead of Resend
2026-10-27

Resend failed every send from 2026-10-26 14:20 UTC on, so "Set up your
password" and organization invitation mails never arrived. The Go origin
now sends through Cloudflare Email Sending instead.

The container can't hold a Workers binding, so it POSTs each mail to
http://mail.internal/send (MAIL_RELAY_URL, set in container.ts). The edge
Worker intercepts that hostname for the container (outboundByHost) and
sends it with its EMAIL send_email binding, restricted to
onboarding@ordnary.com (cloudflare/edge/src/mail.ts). The hostname only
resolves inside the container network, so the relay has no public route.

backend/internal/resend is now backend/internal/mailer, with a 20 second
timeout. RESEND_API_KEY, RESEND_FROM_EMAIL and RESEND_FROM_NAME are gone;
MAIL_FROM_EMAIL and MAIL_FROM_NAME replace the last two.
