Overlay when the API is slow or unreachable, no more login flash
2026-10-29

Opening the dashboard after a quiet period often showed a blank blue
screen for seconds: the API container sleeps after 10 minutes
(sleepAfter in cloudflare/edge/src/container.ts) and has to start first.
Worse, AuthContext treated every failed GET /api/me as signed out, so a
timeout or 5xx during that start sent people with a valid session to
/login, which flashed by until the next page load succeeded.

Now only a 4xx from /api/me means signed out. Network errors, timeouts
(15 s per attempt) and 5xx are retried after 1, 2 and 4 s, then every 15 s.
ConnectionOverlay shows over the loading screen: "taking longer than
usual" after 6 s, "currently unreachable" once the retries ran out, with
Try again, the status page, webmail, and MAINTENANCE_NOTICE when set.
It continues by itself as soon as the API answers.

The beta has the same fix: Session.load() in beta/src/app/session.ts
retries instead of returning false, and ConnectionOverlay sits in the root
component over the guard's wait.
