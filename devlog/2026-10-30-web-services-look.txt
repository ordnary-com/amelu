2.0 dashboard in the Ordnary Web Services look
2026-10-30

The beta's sign-in (beta/src/app/login) now looks like the Web Services
sign-in in ordnary-console: a wide black panel with an animated dither
in Amelu blue (login/dither-shader.ts, Paper Shaders, @paper-design/shaders
added to beta) and the white Amelu icon, the sign-in on the right, a
footer bar with the amelu.org legal links. Always dark, #171d25 with a
#0051fe pill button. The photo carousel is gone.

Sign-in is direct now: no more "We found your Amelu account, continue?"
step. With a session /login goes straight on, and the loginPending flag in
session.ts and auth.guard.ts is removed.

The whole beta uses Google Sans Text and Display (was Source Sans 3) and
the Web Services dark palette in styles.css: page #14191f, cards and
chrome #171d25, lines #43464f, text #dedee3. Light mode keeps its greys
with #0051fe as the blue.
