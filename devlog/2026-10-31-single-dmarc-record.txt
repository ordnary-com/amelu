amelu.org has one DMARC record again
2026-10-31

_dmarc.amelu.org had two TXT records, "p=reject; rua=postmaster@" and
"p=quarantine; ... rua/ruf=dmarc-reports@". With two records receivers
ignore DMARC for the domain altogether. The reject record is deleted, and
the remaining one is now:

v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; pct=100; rua=mailto:postmaster@amelu.org

dmarc-reports@amelu.org doesn't exist on marduk (550 5.1.2), so the
reports go to postmaster@, which does. ruf (forensic reports) is dropped.
