Sender guard pauses cold outreach on marduk automatically
2026-11-01

Gmail kept rate-limiting marduk after the first four domains were paused,
because new cold senders kept coming (outreach.eur3duty.com,
workflowflare.com, both paused by hand today). The 200/hour outbound
throttle doesn't catch them: they send 10 to 60 mails a day.

ops/marduk/amelu-sender-guard.py now runs hourly on marduk
(/etc/cron.d/amelu-sender-guard, log /var/log/amelu-sender-guard.log).
Per sender domain over 24 hours it pauses emailSend when: 40+ recipient
domains; or 10+ with 3+ "user unknown" refusals; or 10+ with 5+ policy
refusals; or 8+ with 25+ Gmail "unsolicited" refusals. Backtested on 28
Oct to 1 Nov: catches all five known senders, a day before the Gmail
limit for two of them, and no normal customer. The customer gets the
sending-paused mail, abuse@amelu.org a summary; backups and reasons are in
/var/lib/amelu-sender-guard/state.json, exceptions in
/etc/amelu-sender-guard/allow.txt.
