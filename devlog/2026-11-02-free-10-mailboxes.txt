2026-11-02

Migration 0033_free_mailboxes_10 raises plan_tiers.max_mailboxes_per_domain
for 'free' from 3 to 10. Domains stay at 1. The limit is enforced in the
mailbox handlers through Store.GetPlanTier, so it applies to existing Free
organizations as soon as the API restarts with the migration. The website
(amelu-landing) says 10 in the FAQ, the pricing table and the FAQ structured
