Outgoing mail limits per plan
2026-11-02

Free 150, Go 1,000 and Pro 3,000 messages a day per sender domain
(plan_tiers.max_outgoing_per_day, migration 0034). The API keeps three
Stalwart x:MtaOutboundThrottle rules in sync, described "Amelu plan: <tier>,
<n> a day per sender domain": go and pro list their domains and enabled
alias domains, free matches every sender except those and amelu.org /
ordnary.com. Sync runs at startup, hourly, and after the Stripe webhook,
an admin plan change, a new domain or a new domain alias
(handlers/sending_limits.go); unchanged rules are not rewritten, changed
ones are followed by ReloadSettings. Over the limit Stalwart holds mail in
the queue, nothing bounces. The 200 an hour per domain rule and the 140 an
hour noez cap stay. Rule shape checked on marduk with a disabled test rule
(created, read back, destroyed).
