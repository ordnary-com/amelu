Webmail slow and "Connection to storage server failed"
2026-11-02

Since the noez IPs became A records of marduk.mx.amelu.org on 1 November
(5.230.173.85-87, for customer SPF via "mx"), Roundcube on marduk picked
one of four addresses for every IMAP connection. The three GRE addresses
time out from the host itself, so three of four logins and refreshes hung
for 10 seconds and failed with "Could not connect to
ssl://marduk.mx.amelu.org:993: Connection timed out" in the Roundcube log.

Fix: extra_hosts "marduk.mx.amelu.org:141.95.52.66" on the roundcube
service in /opt/amelu-webmail/compose.yml, container recreated. Connects by
name went from 2/10 to 10/10, no IMAP errors since. TLS still verifies
against the name. Backup: compose.yml.bak-2026-11-02. Anything else on
marduk that dials that name needs the same pin.
