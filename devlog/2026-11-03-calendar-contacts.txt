Calendar and contacts for every mailbox
2026-11-03

Stalwart's User role already allows CalDAV and CardDAV (davCal* and davCard*
permissions), so every mailbox had a calendar and an address book nobody knew
about. Checked with a throwaway amelu.org mailbox (deleted afterwards):
/.well-known/caldav and carddav redirect to /dav/cal and /dav/card, the
principal is /dav/pal/<address>/, and PUT of an event and a vCard returned
201 and read back. Now: "Calendar and contacts" in Enabled services and the
domain's default services (mailboxes.may_dav, domains.default_may_dav,
migration 0036), which disables the 25 DAV permissions in
stalwart.DAVPermissions. mayDav is optional in the request so the 1.x
dashboard's services form can't switch it off. The Usage instructions page
lists the CalDAV and CardDAV addresses and per-app notes; the Apple profile
and the Thunderbird config from amelu-autoconfig include both. Webmail
(Roundcube) has no calendar.
