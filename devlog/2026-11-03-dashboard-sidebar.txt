Dashboard sidebar reworked
2026-11-03

"Email Domains" is "Domains" everywhere (sidebar, titles, breadcrumbs). The
Domains and My Organization categories end in a chevron and fold away;
clicking the category opens its page and unfolds it, clicking it again on
that page folds it. Domains starts open, My Organization folded (and opens by
itself on its sub-pages); remembered in localStorage amelu-sidebar-collapsed.
The page you're on, and the one under the pointer, is in full-strength text
instead of a highlight block. Domains show a status dot (ok, warn, bad) in
place of the globe, and a small count after "Domains" when more exist than
the three shown. "New ..." items are no longer green (unreadable on the light
and blue sidebars) but plain text with a plus. The domain column is headed
with the domain's name and split into groups with thin dividers.
