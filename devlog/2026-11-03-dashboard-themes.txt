Dashboard themes and accent colours
2026-11-03

Appearance (and the account menu, as swatches) now offers Dark, Dim, Black,
Deep, Light, White, Sepia, High contrast and High contrast dark, plus System,
which follows the device between dark and light and picks the matching high
contrast theme under prefers-contrast: more. Six accent colours (blue,
purple, green, teal, orange, pink) work with every theme; links, the badge
and the coloured sidebars derive from --accent with color-mix. On <html>:
"dark" for every dark theme, theme-<name> and accent-<name>, set by
theme.ts and again before first paint in index.html. Sidebar text on the
chrome is a --chrome-ink token instead of hardcoded white, so White and Sepia
can have a light sidebar. The hidden radio inputs on Appearance made the
whole page taller than the screen (sr-only escaping the scroll pane); the
labels are relative now. Appearance, API Keys and Plans load lazily to stay
under the 500 kB initial budget. Also today: Google Sans without bold.
