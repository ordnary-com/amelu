DNS and Spam filtering use tabs instead of a third sidebar column
2026-11-03

Opening DNS Configuration or Spam Filtering added a third menu column next
to the main sidebar and the domain menu, which left the page content too
narrow. Both sections now show their pages as tabs under the page header
(beta/src/app/section-tabs.ts, the same look as My Account's tabs): DNS
configuration has Overview and Records, Spam filtering has Overview,
Aggressiveness, Sender lists and Recipient denylist. Every page in a section
has the section's title, keeps its own description, and the breadcrumb ends
at the section. menuColumns in shell.ts no longer adds the dns and spam
columns.
