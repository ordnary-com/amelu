Domain page shows sending against the daily limit
2026-11-03

Stalwart doesn't expose its outbound throttle counters, so
ops/marduk/amelu-sending-stats.py counts messages per sender domain on the
remote queue over the last 24 hours from Stalwart's log (as the sender guard
does; bounces and DMARC reports excluded) and posts them every 15 minutes to
POST /internal/jobs/sending-stats, HMAC-signed with its own
SENDING_STATS_SECRET. Each report replaces table sending_stats; the time goes
in sending_stats_report (migration 0035). GET /api/domains/{id}/sending
returns sent, the plan's max_outgoing_per_day and updatedAt; the domain page
shows "Sent in the last 24 hours" with a bar, amber from 80%, red at the
limit. A dry run on marduk takes 0.2 s.
