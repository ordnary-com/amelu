Open in webmail no longer ends in a 522
2026-11-03

The amelu-webmail Worker reached Roundcube as marduk.mx.amelu.org:8443. That
name has four A records: 141.95.52.66 and the three noez GRE IPs, which stay
for customers' SPF "mx". Only marduk's own IP answers on 8443, so about three
in four requests hung until Cloudflare gave up with Error 522 (GitHub #4).

New DNS-only record webmail-origin.mx.amelu.org -> 141.95.52.66 only, with
its own Let's Encrypt certificate (certbot standalone, cert-name
webmail-origin.mx.amelu.org; the webmail-nginx.sh hook reloads nginx, the
Stalwart hook ignores this lineage). nginx in /opt/amelu-webmail uses it, the
Worker's ORIGIN points at it. 12 of 12 test requests returned 200 after the
deploy. docs/WEBMAIL.md says why it must not go back to the marduk name.
