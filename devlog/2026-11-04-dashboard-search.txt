Search for any page, domain or mailbox in the dashboard
2026-11-04

A field in the top bar (beta/src/app/search), opened with a click,
Cmd/Ctrl+K or "/", finds account pages, every domain and its pages, and
every mailbox and its pages. Each page has keywords for what people type
instead of its name, so "imap" finds every mailbox's IMAP and SMTP settings
(the Usage instructions page), "dkim" the DNS pages and "invoice" the
invoices. A label that starts with the query ranks first, and things in the
domain you're on come before the rest. Mailboxes are fetched once, the
first time the search opens, for at most 25 domains. Suggested by mihal.pl,
who couldn't find the IMAP settings. New domain and the activity log now
load lazily to keep the initial bundle under 500 kB.
