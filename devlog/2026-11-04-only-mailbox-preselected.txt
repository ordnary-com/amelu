The only mailbox is preselected as an alias destination
2026-11-04

On New address alias and New pattern rewrite, "Deliver to" started at
"Choose a mailbox" even when the domain had a single mailbox, so it had to be
picked every time (suggested by mihal.pl). The shared amelu-mailbox-field in
beta/src/app/routing/shared.ts now takes the only mailbox when there is just
one, unless the form already chose one (?mailbox= from a mailbox page).
