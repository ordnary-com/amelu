Usage instructions list the apps for your device
2026-11-04

The mailbox Usage instructions page had "Set up automatically" with only
the Apple profile and the Outlook SRV record. A customer pointed out that
Thunderbird, Fantastical, BusyCal and eM Client work with our CalDAV and
CardDAV too. The section is now "Set up your apps": a list of nine apps
(Apple profile, Fantastical, BusyCal, eM Client, Thunderbird, DAVx5, the
Android mail app, Outlook, Evolution), each with what it sets up (mail,
calendar, contacts) and one line on how. The browser's platform (Mac/iOS,
Windows, Android, Linux, from the user agent) decides which come first;
"Show all apps" adds the rest. The separate calendar apps card is gone.
