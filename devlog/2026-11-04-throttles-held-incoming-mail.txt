Outbound limits no longer hold incoming mail
2026-11-04

Stalwart applies x:MtaOutboundThrottle rules to the local queue as well, so
mail arriving for our own mailboxes counted against the outbound limits. When
the noez cap (140 an hour in total) filled up, as it did at 00:00 and 01:00
UTC from the nightly DMARC reports, every incoming message waited for the
next full hour: "delivery.rate-limit-exceeded ... queueName = "local" ...
nextRetry = 01:00:00Z". The per-sender-domain plan limits did the same, so
for example allegro.pl notifications to all customers shared one free-plan
bucket of 150 a day. Reported by mihal.pl: no mail for an hour, then all at
once. All five rules now start with "queue_name == 'local'" -> false, set by
hand on marduk at 10:40 UTC (backup /root/throttles-backup-2026-11-04.json)
and in OutboundThrottle.match() so the API's sync writes the same.
