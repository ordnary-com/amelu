Outbound mail is IPv4 only, after marduk's IPv6 address got a Spamhaus CSS listing
2026-11-06

The Stalwart route "mx" had ipLookupStrategy v4ThenV6. While the noez tunnel
was broken (see 2026-11-06-noez-ip-rules-lost.txt) every IPv4 attempt from
out1-3 failed, so Stalwart fell back to marduk's own IPv6 address
2001:41d0:701:1100::74d4: 250 connections on 5 November and about 50 on
6 November, mostly to qq.com and gmail.com, outside the IP pools and their
caps. Spamhaus listed that address in CSS, and rzone.de (Strato) and
mailbox.org rejected mail from it ("IP listed on RBL ... Listed by CSS").
The route is now v4Only (x:MtaRoute i7dci0r2adab), so outbound mail never
leaves over IPv6; the sending IPs are all IPv4. The CSS listing still has to
be removed at check.spamhaus.org.
