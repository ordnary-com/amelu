From 2026-11-05 06:02 UTC every delivery from out1-3 (5.230.173.85-87) timed
out after about 134 s ("Connection timed out (os error 110)"): new domains,
amelu.org itself and all mail to Gmail. 354 messages from 38 sender domains
sat in the queue. The GRE tunnel itself was fine; systemd-networkd had
restarted (systemd upgrade) and, with ManageForeignRoutingPolicyRules on by
default, deleted the "from 5.230.173.x lookup 20" rules gre-noez adds, so
those IPs left over ens3 and OVH dropped them.
The rules are back (deliveries from all three IPs connect again) and
/etc/systemd/networkd.conf.d/10-keep-gre-noez.conf (copy in ops/marduk)
turns off networkd's cleanup of foreign rules and routes.
