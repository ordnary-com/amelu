DNS check accepts a merged SPF record
2026-11-06

The DNS check compared the SPF TXT record byte for byte with Stalwart's
"v=spf1 mx -all". Domain Connect's SPFM record type (and anyone who also
sends through Google or similar) produces a merged record such as
"v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com mx ~all", which showed as a mismatch.
checkTXT in internal/dnscheck now counts an SPF record as matched when it
contains every mechanism from the expected record; the all qualifier is
ignored.
