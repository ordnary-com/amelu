Free plan has no daily sending limit anymore
2026-11-07

Migration 0037 sets plan_tiers.max_outgoing_per_day to 0 for free, and
0 now means no daily cap: SyncSendingLimits keeps the "Amelu plan: free"
throttle but disables it ("free, no daily limit (disabled)"), so turning a
cap back on is only a plan_tiers change. Abuse stays covered by the hourly
per-domain throttle (200 an hour) and the sender guard.
Reason: the free rule's exemption list didn't reliably exempt amelu.org's own
mail (devlog 2026-11-07-reports-out-of-plan-limits.txt), and the support
reply of 23 October already told free customers there is no fixed daily
limit.
The 2.0 dashboard's domain page shows "No daily limit on your plan" without
a bar when the limit is 0, instead of a full red bar.
The landing site still lists "150 a day" for Free.
