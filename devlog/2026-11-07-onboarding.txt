First-login onboarding: a few questions and the first domain
2026-11-07

The owner of a new organization goes to /welcome (onboardingGuard) on first
sign-in: organization name, what Amelu is for (personal, business, clients,
nonprofit, other), how many people, and how they found us (search, social
media, Reddit or a forum, a friend, a review, a comparison site, an AI
assistant, Ordnary, somewhere else, with a free text field for some). Step 3
sends them to /domains/new?name=... so the regular page creates the domain
with its standard addresses and DNS help. "Skip for now" and "I'll do this
later" also mark the account done.
POST /api/onboarding stores the answers in onboarding_answers (fixed values,
so they can be counted) and sets customers.onboarded_at; /api/me reports
"onboarded". Migration 0039 sets onboarded_at for every existing account, so
only new accounts see the flow. Invited team members don't (owner only).
