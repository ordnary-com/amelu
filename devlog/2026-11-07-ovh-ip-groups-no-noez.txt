Outbound IPs in three groups on OVH only; noez tunnel dropped
2026-11-07

amelu-ip-pools no longer uses the noez GRE IPs. Three Stalwart connection
strategies, picked by sender domain: system (amelu.org, out4), newdomains
(domains younger than 7 days, out6 for now) and trusted (everyone else,
out5). The Gmail-to-out1 rule and the 140/h tunnel cap are gone (the cap's
throttle is disabled); one warm-up cap covers all sending IPs.
Eight more OVH additional IPs were ordered. The names out1-out3 now point
at new IPs; out1/out2 join trusted, out3/out7/out8 join newdomains, and
out9-out11 are spares (out11 is 51.89.76.0, last to use). An IP only joins
its group once it is bound on marduk, is an A record of marduk.mx.amelu.org
and has matching PTR and A records, so new IPs switch on by themselves.
DNS: the three 5.230.173.x records are gone from marduk.mx, out1-out3 were
repointed, out7-out11 created, each outN with "v=spf1 a -all". Done through
a Cloudflare API token limited to DNS edit on amelu.org (keychain "amelu
cloudflare dns").
Still to do: PTRs at OVH, binding the new IPs on marduk permanently, then
stopping gre-noez.
Later the same day: all 11 IPs are in /etc/netplan/60-amelu-sending-ips.yaml
(copy in ops/marduk; out4-6 were already in it, so they survive a reboot)
and were added live with ip addr replace, no netplan apply. PTRs set in the
OVH panel; out1, out2, out3, out7 and out8 added to marduk.mx. amelu-ip-pools
only uses an IP 2 hours after it first appears in marduk.mx, because
receivers cache that record (TTL 3600) for their SPF check. The old out1-3
and ovh connection strategies are deleted; gre-noez is stopped and its UFW
rule removed at 15:40 UTC by a one-off systemd timer (/root/noez-shutdown.sh).
