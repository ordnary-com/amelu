DMARC/TLS reports no longer count against amelu.org's plan limit
2026-11-07

Since 3 November the free plan rule ("Amelu plan: free, 150 a day per sender
domain") held back amelu.org's DMARC and TLS reports, 1605 today, although
amelu.org is in the exempt paid list. The reports and bounces Stalwart makes
itself don't carry the customer's sender_domain when throttles run, so they
fell through to the free rule; customer mail was not affected (trdlo.online,
Go, sent 183 on 4 November untouched).
Every plan rule (internal/stalwart/throttles.go) and the manual "Outbound per
sender domain: 200 per hour" rule now first skip source == 'report' or 'dsn'.
Reports get their own "DMARC and TLS reports cap", 120 an hour in total, so
they can't eat the sending IPs' warm-up rate; amelu-ip-pools manages both.
Found out on the way, per the Stalwart docs and a test with throwaway
throttles: throttles keyed on the sender domain run once per message before
routing, so rcpt_domain is empty there and the is_local_domain(rcpt_domain)
"skip local delivery" condition never matches. Left in place; local
delivery from a free domain over its limit is still held.
