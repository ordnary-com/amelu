Sender guard counts every recipient, reports volume instead of pausing on it
2026-11-07

ops/marduk/amelu-sender-guard.py (live on marduk, old copy at
/usr/local/bin/amelu-sender-guard.bak-20261107):
- It counted only the first address of to = ["a", "b", ...], so a message to
  20 people counted as one recipient. Every address is counted now.
- postmaster@ was skipped entirely, because the server's own bounces and
  reports leave from it. It is now skipped only for those; mail a person
  submitted (queue.authenticated-message-queued) counts.
- The Gmail "unsolicited" rule needs 10 recipient domains, like the others
  (was 8), so the fuller count doesn't newly catch grupocomunicarte.com.ar.
- New: 100+ distinct recipients in 24h is only reported to abuse@amelu.org,
  once per 7 days per domain. Volume never pauses anyone on its own.
- A failed notice mail no longer crashes the run (it did twice, because
  abuse@amelu.org doesn't exist).
- --backtest N replays the rules over the last N log days. Over 30 days it
  pauses the same known abusers as before and reports catxiaolan.top,
  tollsett.com and nrmasa.com for review.
