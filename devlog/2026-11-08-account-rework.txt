My Account in the new style
2026-11-08

The /account pages in the beta open with a profile banner in AccountLayout,
like the Overview greeting: bg-hero with the accent warp dither, the
Ordnary picture (amelu-avatar, 64px with a soft ring), the name at 32px in
normal weight, and email, organization and role pill under it. The
separate avatar card on Overview is gone. Profile gains an Organization row
and shows the permissions level as a pill. Rows use divide-y. Two-step
verification has a small outlined Manage button instead of a text link.
The Ordnary account card gets the dither, a key icon and an outlined
button. Terminate account uses UI.dangerOutline instead of the hard-coded
solid #d91515. Appearance splits Theme and Accent colour into two cards.
Theme options get a round check and the accent ring, and the chosen
accent swatch carries a check. API keys get a key tile per key, the prefix
as a mono chip, divide-y rows and a dither on "Using a key". The Copy
button for a new key is the standard primary button, and the name
placeholder says "For example:" instead of "eg.".
