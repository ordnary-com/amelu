Add domain page in the new style
2026-11-08

/domains/new in the beta reworks the form. The domain field is 48px tall
with 16px text, on a card with the accent warp dither from the Overview
banner in its top right. It shows a green check once the input is a valid
domain. Under it, one line changes with what's typed: the www. warning, the
cleaned name, "Addresses will look like you@...", or a tip that a website
or address works too. The nameserver choice ("How do you want to handle
DNS?") is two cards with a round check indicator instead of bare radios.
The radios stay as sr-only inputs. The badges are sentence case pills
(Easiest, Soon), no longer uppercase. "Create the standard addresses" is a
switch. Under it, admin, postmaster and abuse are rows instead of chips:
an icon, the address with the domain in muted text, and what it's for
("Where other mail servers report delivery problems"). Each row has a green
check while the switch is on and dims when it's off. The submit button
names the domain ("Add acme.com"). What happens next is a timeline with a
connecting line. The plan usage card gets a See plans link at the limit.
The success view is unchanged.
