Amelu nameservers on the add-domain page (built, off)
2026-11-08

"Amelu nameservers" on /domains/new was greyed out "Soon". It now creates a
Cloudflare zone in the Ordnary account per domain (internal/cfdns, table
domain_dns_zones, migration 0040), copies the domain's current records
with the dns_records/scan endpoint and writes Stalwart's mail records with
the comment "Managed by Amelu for email" (read-only in the dashboard). New
endpoints: GET /api/nameservers, GET/POST /api/domains/{id}/nameservers,
GET/POST/PUT/DELETE /api/domains/{id}/dns-records. New "DNS zone" tab.
Deleting a domain deletes its zone.

What's blocking it: POST /accounts/{id}/custom_ns answers "This feature is
not enabled for your account" (1002), so ns1/ns2.amelu.org needs Cloudflare
to enable account custom nameservers. AMELU_NS_SET=0 runs it on the pair
Cloudflare assigns per zone. Off until AMELU_DNS_API_TOKEN is set on the
edge Worker. A subdomain test zone (nstest.amelu.org) was refused, so it is
untested against Cloudflare. See docs/AMELU_NAMESERVERS.md.
