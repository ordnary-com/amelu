Appearance page with a live preview
2026-11-08

/account/appearance in the beta is a two-column page now. The options are
on the left. A live preview of the dashboard sits on the right (sticky on
xl): top bar, menu with the active item, a greeting, a domain card with an
Active pill and an accent bar, a primary button and a link. It is drawn
with the page's own colour tokens (bg-chrome, bg-page, bg-surface,
bg-accent), so it shows whatever theme and accent are picked, System
included. System is its own wide option at the top and says what it
resolves to right now. The other themes are grouped under Dark, Light and
High contrast. Their miniatures show menu lines, a heading, a card and a
button in the chosen accent. Accent colours are larger swatches with a
check, in a six-column grid. "Reset to default" (Dark, Blue) shows only
when something else is picked.
