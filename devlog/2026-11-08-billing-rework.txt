Billing in the new style
2026-11-08

The /billing pages in the beta open with a plan banner in BillingLayout:
bg-hero with the accent warp dither, "<Plan> plan" at 32px in normal
weight, the price line and the subscription status pill, and Change plan
top right. Overview swaps the Subscription card for three stat tiles:
domains used with a usage bar (red when over, with a warning alert),
mailboxes per domain and payment method. Status and price move to a small
Subscription list in the sidebar, and Manage subscription gets the dither.
Recent invoices are divide-y rows with a receipt tile and a nudging
chevron, without the hover underline. On Plans, the period switch marks
the active option in accent, the current plan is a "Current plan" pill
instead of an uppercase tag, prices are 32px normal weight, the yearly
saving is a green pill, features get check icons, and Cancel plan uses
UI.dangerOutline. Invoices get a tinted table head, divide-y rows, small
outlined View and PDF buttons and a dithered About card.
