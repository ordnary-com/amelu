Delivery charts: 14 days per domain, a sparkline per mailbox
2026-11-08

The dashboard only knew "sent in the last 24 hours". ops/marduk/amelu-sending-stats.py
now also reports history: per sender domain and UTC day the messages sent,
recipients delivered, deferred (4xx) and rejected (5xx), per sender address
the messages sent, and per domain the five most common 5xx reasons of the
last 7 days, with trace ids and long tokens with digits replaced by # so
the same reason groups (163.com appends one to every reply). Finished days
are counted once into /var/lib/amelu-sending-stats/days; a run takes 0.5 s.
Live on marduk (backup /root/amelu-sending-stats.backup-2026-11-08); the
API ignores the new field until the backend is deployed.

Backend: tables sending_history, sending_history_senders, sending_reasons
(migration 0042), written with unnest, older than 30 days dropped; GET
/api/domains/{id}/sending/history returns all 14 days. Dashboard: a stacked
bar chart on the domain page (delivered, deferred, rejected, with hover,
legend totals and a screen-reader table) and the rejection reasons with a
plain-language hint (delivery/reasons.ts); a sparkline of sent mail per
mailbox in the mailbox list.
