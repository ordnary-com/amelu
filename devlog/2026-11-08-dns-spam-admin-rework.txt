DNS, spam and domain settings pages in the new style
2026-11-08

The rest of the domain pages in the beta follow the reworked ones. DNS
configuration shows "N of M records verified" large, with the four checks
as a list of check or cross circles instead of uppercase tiles, and the
zone import card gets the dither. DNS records, the nameservers card, the
DNS zone, spam filtering and its sub pages use divide-y rows, standard
pills (In use, Waiting, For email, Listed), the larger switches and count
chips on their headings. On DNS zone, record types become mono chips and
Edit and Delete become small buttons. Transfer, deactivate and delete put
the consequences in a list inside the card and use a red outline button.
The solid red button is only for the final confirm in the dialog. That
style is now UI.dangerOutline in ui.ts. Placeholders say "For example:"
instead of "eg.".
