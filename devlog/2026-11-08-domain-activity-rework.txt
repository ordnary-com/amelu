Domain Recent activity page in the new style
2026-11-08

/domains/:id/activity in the beta replaces its filter buttons and the
Summary card with one row of filter chips (All, Domain, Mailboxes, Spam
filtering), each with its colour dot and count. Day headings are 14px bold
with a count instead of 12px uppercase. The events are a timeline: one thin
line through a card per day, a round tile per event with an icon for its
category (globe, mail, shield, tinted like the dot), the message, and the
time with the full date and seconds on hover. Changes by support show a
"By Amelu support" pill. "Looking for who did it?" gets the accent dither
and an icon like the DNS help card, plus an "Open the activity log" button.
The Recent activity card on the domain overview uses the same timeline in
small: an accent dot per event on a thin line.
