Domain cards: status and DNS health in one badge
2026-11-08

The domain card (Overview and Domains page) had a square status badge, a
separate 0-100 ring and square DNS badges with a dot. Now one round badge
holds a small progress ring, the status and the percentage ("DNS pending ·
60%"), in the status's colour, except that an active domain with a record
that doesn't check out turns amber. While the DNS check runs only the empty
ring and the status show. The MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC badges are round too,
with a check, a cross or dots for not checked instead of a coloured dot, and
wrap on narrow cards.
