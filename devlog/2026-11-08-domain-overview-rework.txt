Domain overview page in the new style
2026-11-08

/domains/:id in the beta opens with a banner like the Overview greeting:
bg-hero with the accent warp dither fading from the right, the breadcrumb
and the New alias and New mailbox buttons on top, and the domain name at
36px in normal weight. Under it is the status badge from the domain cards
(ring plus DNS %, amber when an active domain has a failing record) with
the added and verified dates. The four stat tiles get a 26px normal-weight
number, a chevron and no underline on hover. Mailboxes also gets a thin
usage bar against the plan limit. The address filter matches the Domains
page (own × button, Escape clears, accent focus ring), and the
All/Mailboxes/Aliases switch marks the active tab in accent. Rows use
divide-y, no underline on hover, and the chevron nudges right. Mailbox
status badges are sentence case pills. The DNS card drops the uppercase
labels and OK/WRONG badges: a filled check or cross circle, the record
name and what it does, and Verified, Wrong or Not checked on the right.
