Domains page: cleaner filter field and sort select
2026-11-08

The sort select on /domains in the beta drew the browser's own arrow. It is
now appearance-none inside a bordered label with a muted "Sort" prefix
("Sort  Your order") and the chevronDown icon from icon.ts on the right. It
is still a native select, so the keyboard and screen readers work as before,
and its options get bg-surface so the list isn't white in dark mode. The
filter field hides the native search cancel button and shows its own ×
once something is typed. Escape clears the filter. Both controls get
hover:border-ink/20 and the same accent focus ring.
