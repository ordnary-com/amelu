Sending IPs, What's new, status, support and the standalone pages
2026-11-08

The remaining pages in the beta follow the new style. Sending IPs shows
three stat tiles, then one divide-y card of addresses with a mono IP chip
and a copy icon on each. What's new is a timeline grouped by month with a
Latest pill, and each entry now links to its full post (entry.link, which
the page never used). Service status gets divide-y service rows with dots
and pills, impact dots on incidents and skeleton rows while loading. It
used to say "No services listed." while still loading. Contact support
gets numbered "What to include" rows and link cards. Terminate account
moves the consequences into the card and uses UI.dangerOutline instead of
the hard-coded #d91515. The bell dropdown gets an unread count chip,
tinted alert tiles and an accent unread dot. Not found and the invite and
reset token pages get a subtle dither and no uppercase. The tab titles now
match the headings: "Contact support · Amelu", "Service status · Amelu".
