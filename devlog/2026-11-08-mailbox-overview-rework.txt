Mailbox overview, activity, logs and usage in the new style
2026-11-08

MailboxLayout in the beta replaces its page header with a banner like the
domain overview's. It has bg-hero with the dither, a breadcrumb up to the
mailbox, and Open in webmail top right. The address is at 34px in normal
weight, with the status pill, display name and created date under it. The
mailbox overview gets four linked stat tiles (services on with a bar,
forwards, internal access only, expires). Details rows are clickable as a
whole with a chevron. Suspend uses UI.dangerOutline, and the mail app card
gets the dither. The separate Created card is gone, since the banner shows
the date. Mailbox activity is a timeline with filter chips (All, Mailbox,
Sign-in and access, Delivery, grouped from the mailbox.* event types) and
support pills. The log tables get a tinted head, divide-y rows and mono
addresses. Usage uses divide-y lists, pills for what each app covers, a
bordered mono box for the Outlook record and a dithered "Signing in" card.
