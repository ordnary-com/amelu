Domain mailbox pages in the new style
2026-11-08

The domain-level mailbox pages in the beta follow the reworked pages.
Mailboxes gets the new filter field, divide-y rows without hover underline,
a stat tile with a usage bar for the plan limit, and the dither on "Mailbox
or alias?" with an "Add an alias" button. On New mailbox, the two password
methods are option cards with a round check and What happens next is a
timeline. Import mailboxes and Storage show Created and Nearly full as
sentence-case pills instead of uppercase tags. Storage gets a "By mailbox"
heading with a count and the large stat number for the domain total.
Default services uses the larger switches, plus a usage bar for how many
services are on. That line said "N of 5" while there are six services
(ManageSieve too), so it could read "6 of 5". It now counts the settings
themselves.
