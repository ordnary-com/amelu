Mailbox settings pages in the new style
2026-11-08

The 15 mailbox settings pages in the beta (internal access, password,
services, delegation, filters, forwarding, identities, delete, expiration,
limits, listing settings, notes) follow the new style. Each is a card with
sentence-case sections and the save button in a footer behind a full-width
line, often with an "Unsaved changes" hint. Lists are divide-y rows with
icon tiles, and one help card per page gets the dither. switch-row.ts is the
standard switch now, and the cards around it use divide-y. Password checks
the 8-character minimum live. Filters is one numbered list with inline
editing, and "Stop there" is a switch. Edit is now off while another filter
is open or saving. Before, it silently threw away the open filter's
unsaved changes. Expiration and limits show their current values as stat
tiles. Delete mailbox puts the consequences in the card and uses
UI.dangerOutline, with solid red only in the confirm. Placeholders say "For
example:".
