Top bar: a notification bell
2026-11-08

Problems only showed on the page they belong to. A bell next to the account
menu (beta/src/app/notifications) now lists what needs attention, worked
out in the browser from what the dashboard already loads: a domain that
failed or isn't active yet (link to its DNS page), a DNS record that's
wrong on an active domain (needsAttention), a domain at 80% or more of its
daily sending limit (GET /api/domains/{id}/sending, first 10 domains),
mailboxes at 85% of their storage limit (the new storage endpoint, first 5
domains) and open invoices. The badge counts unread ones; "Mark all as
read" stores the current ids in localStorage (amelu.notices.read), and an
id changes when the situation does (sending near vs full, how many
mailboxes are full), so a new problem shows as unread again. Empty list:
"All good". The initial bundle is now 519 kB against the 500 kB budget
(a warning, not an error).
