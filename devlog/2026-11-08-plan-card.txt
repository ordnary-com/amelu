Overview: a clearer Your plan card
2026-11-08

The plan card had an Upgrade button at the top and another at the bottom,
and only listed domains and mailboxes. Now the plan name is larger with how
it's paid under it ("No charge", "Billed yearly" or "Billed monthly"), and
the top button is gone on Free; paid plans get a Manage link to /billing.
The limits are one list: domains with a thin bar, mailboxes per domain, and
the daily sending limit per domain (150, 1,000 or 3,000 by plan id, the
same numbers as the API's plan tiers). Over the domain limit, one line with
an alert icon says what to do. On Free, what Go adds sits in a light accent
box with a single full-width "Upgrade to Go" button. Loading shows
placeholders instead of "Loading...".
