The Black Friday card is a one-line alert
2026-11-08

The promo banner (beta/src/app/promo-banner) was a tinted card with an
uppercase label and two boxed codes, unlike anything else on the page. It's
now one line in a plain surface box with a star icon: "Black Week until 23
November. 30% off your first year on Go or Pro with BLACKWEEK30, or 15% off
your first 3 months paying monthly with WEEK15. See plans". The codes are
small monospace chips that copy on click. An × hides it, remembered per
deal in localStorage (amelu.promo.dismissed.<yearly code>), so the next
phase's deal shows again. The plans page keeps that line without the link.
On free plans the deal now sits at the bottom of the main sidebar menu
instead of on Overview (PromoBanner variant "sidebar"), as part of the menu
rather than a card: the Paper Shaders dither in the menu's ink colour
(var(--chrome-ink), 2 px dots), rising from the bottom and fading into the
sidebar, with the deal, the yearly code to copy, See plans and × on top. The
main menu column is a flex column so it stays at the bottom.
