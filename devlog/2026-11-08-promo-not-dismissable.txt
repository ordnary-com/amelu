The deal card can't be dismissed anymore
2026-11-08

The Black Friday card at the bottom of the sidebar and the one-line deal on
the plans page both lose their × button. PromoBanner no longer has
dismiss(), the dismissed signal or the localStorage key
amelu.promo.dismissed.<yearly code>, so a card someone closed earlier shows
again. The card still disappears by itself after the campaign's last day
(activePromo() returns nothing), and the sidebar one only shows on the free
plan.
