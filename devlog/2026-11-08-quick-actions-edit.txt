Quick actions: rearrange, remove and add them
2026-11-08

The six quick actions on Overview were fixed. "Edit" next to the heading
now makes the cards draggable (Angular CDK drop list, mixed orientation
over flex-wrap tiles sized like the 1/2/3/6 column grid), gives each an ×
to remove it, and lists the others to add: Add a domain, New mailbox, DNS
records, Invoices, Plans, Webmail branding, What's new, Sending IPs and
Service status, next to the original six. "Done" or Escape ends editing,
"Reset" brings back the default six. The order is stored per browser in
localStorage (amelu.quick-actions.v1). Actions under a domain (Mailboxes,
Recent Activity, New mailbox, DNS records) wait until there is a domain and
keep their place in the order meanwhile. The cards keep their old look.
