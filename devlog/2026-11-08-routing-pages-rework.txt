Routing pages in the new style
2026-11-08

The domain routing pages in the beta (address aliases, domain aliases,
pattern rewrites, catch-all, Bcc. captures, their New forms and the alias
CSV import) follow the reworked pages. List rows use divide-y instead of a
border per row. The address alias filter works like the Domains page (own
× button, Escape clears, accent focus ring). The Related rows no longer
underline and their chevron nudges right. Each page's help card in the
sidebar gets the accent dither with an icon, and its link becomes an
outlined button. Side-card separators run full width. The mailbox picker
in routing/shared.ts hides the browser arrow for our chevron. On the CSV
import result, the Rows heading gets a count, and the result column shows
Created or Failed pills, with the error under Failed.
