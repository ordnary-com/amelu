Sidebar: the second menu column gets headings instead of an uppercase label
2026-11-08

The domain and mailbox menu column in the beta shell opened with the domain
name as an 11px uppercase label, and split its groups with bare 1px lines.
It now starts with a small "Domain" or "Mailbox" line over the domain name or
mailbox address in 15px. The mailbox address comes from GET
/api/mailboxes/:id, fetched by the shell, because it sits above
MailboxLayout's MailboxContext. MenuEntry.divider became MenuEntry.group, a
named heading in sentence case: Routing, Delivery, Manage and More for a
domain, and Access, Mail handling, Manage and More for a mailbox. The nav's
lg:pr-4 after the second column is gone, and the column has px-1 instead of
pl-1, so its items sit 12px from the border on the left and 12px from the
page on the right.
