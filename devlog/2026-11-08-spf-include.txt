SPF: customers are asked for include:_spf.amelu.org
2026-11-08

New TXT _spf.amelu.org: v=spf1 ip4:141.95.52.66 plus ip4 for out1-out11 -all.
The backend swaps Stalwart's generated "v=spf1 mx -all" for
"v=spf1 include:_spf.amelu.org -all" (stalwart.UseAmeluSPF) in GET
/api/domains/{id}/dns, the zone file download and Amelu-hosted zones.
dnscheck.spfCovers accepts "mx" for the include, so every domain set up
earlier stays verified. A sending IP still has to be an A record of
marduk.mx.amelu.org as long as those "mx" domains exist; amelu-ip-pools
warns when _spf.amelu.org misses a sending IP. Domain Connect's template
still merges "mx". Committed, not deployed yet.
