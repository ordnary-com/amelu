Picking a theme no longer scrolls the whole page
2026-11-08

On /account/appearance, clicking the System option or an accent colour
scrolled the whole document past the app, leaving a grey block of bare page
background at the bottom. The options are labels around sr-only radio
inputs. sr-only is position: absolute, and those labels weren't
positioned, so each input was placed relative to some far-off ancestor.
Focusing it on click made the browser scroll the document to it. The
labels are relative now. The same setup was on the file pickers on
organization webmail (logo upload) and the alias CSV import, so those
labels got relative too.
