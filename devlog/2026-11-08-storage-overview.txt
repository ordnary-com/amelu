Mailboxes: a storage overview per domain
2026-11-08

There was no way to see which mailbox takes how much space. New endpoint
GET /api/domains/{id}/mailboxes/storage (handlers/mailbox_storage.go) joins
the domain's mailboxes with stalwart.DomainStorage, one x:Account/query
filtered on domainId plus one x:Account/get of name, usedDiskQuota and
quotas (filtering on domainId alone was confirmed live on marduk). New page
/domains/:id/mailboxes/storage, linked from the mailbox settings list: a
bar per mailbox, biggest first, size against its limit or, without a limit,
against the biggest mailbox, a "Nearly full" badge from 85%, a Limit link,
and the domain total in the sidebar. formatBytes uses binary units.
