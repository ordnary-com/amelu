Welcome flow as a chat with the setup assistant
2026-11-08

/welcome in the beta was three form steps. It is now a conversation with
"Amelu Assistant": one question at a time, each shown after a short typing
indicator (650ms, none with prefers-reduced-motion). The order is
organization name, use case, team size, source, the optional follow-up on
the source and the first domain. Your answers appear as accent bubbles on
the right and can be changed (Edit) until they're saved. The lines refer
back to earlier answers ("Acme, nice. What will you use Amelu for?").
Choices are quick-reply cards in an even grid in the composer, picked with
a click or keys 1 to 9. Text questions use a pill text box with Send. Every
question can be skipped. The answers still go to POST /api/onboarding
before the domain question, and Add domain still opens /domains/new?name=.
The header shows the assistant, a progress bar with one segment per
question and Skip for now.
