Free mail domains can't be added as a domain anymore
2026-11-09

A customer added hotmail.com as their own domain on 2026-11-05 14:15 UTC,
then created the mailbox puzzleclapper2@hotmail.com at 14:30. Stalwart
treats every domain it hosts as local and doesn't follow MX for it. So
from that moment on, every authenticated Amelu sender got "550 5.1.2
Mailbox does not exist" at RCPT TO for any other @hotmail.com address, and
that mail never reached our outbound queue. A customer (tuyuka.be) found it
with an openssl test. isReservedDomain in handlers/reserved_domains.go only
knew ordnary.com, ordnary.cloud and amelu.org. It now also refuses an exact
list of free mail provider domains (Gmail, Hotmail, Outlook, Live, Yahoo,
iCloud, Proton, GMX and the big Dutch, Belgian and French ISPs), for new
domains and domain aliases alike. Those get the error "this is a public
email provider's domain, add a domain you own instead". The hotmail.com
domain on marduk still has to be removed separately.
