Custom plans that a customer accepts and pays for themselves
2026-11-11

Support can now make a plan for one organization in Airplane (Amelu
customer page, Custom plans, New custom plan): name, domains, mailboxes per
domain, outgoing per day, branded webmail, price and yearly or monthly.
POST /internal/admin/customers/{id}/plan-offers creates a plan_tiers row
with custom = true, a Stripe product and price (lookup key
amelu_custom-xxxx_annual) and a plan_offers row (migration 0044). The link
is app.amelu.org/billing/offer/<id>; it only opens for someone who manages
billing in that organization, and billing/overview shows a banner while an
offer is open. Accepting opens Stripe Checkout with offerId in the metadata;
checkout.session.completed sets the plan and closes the offer. With an
existing subscription the price is swapped instead (always_invoice). Other
organizations never see a custom tier in /api/billing/plans.
