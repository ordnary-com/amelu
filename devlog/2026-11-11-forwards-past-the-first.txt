Mailbox forwarding works for more than one destination
2026-11-11

A mailbox with three forwards (autoforward@dedalosairpark.gr) only ever
forwarded to the first one. The marduk log showed one sieve.send-message
per incoming message, with no error. Stalwart's x:SieveUserInterpreter had
maxRedirects 1 and maxOutMessages 3, and it drops every redirect past the
limit without a word. The forwarding script puts one redirect :copy per
destination in the same script, so the second and third never went out.
The same limit broke a Roundcube filter with two redirects for
triadesolucoes.com earlier today.

Both limits are now 5 on marduk (JMAP x:SieveUserInterpreter/set). Backup
of the old values: /root/sieve-user-interpreter.backup-2026-11-11.json.
POST /api/mailboxes/{id}/forwards now refuses a sixth forward with "a mailbox
can have at most 5 forwards", so the dashboard can't promise more than
Stalwart sends.
