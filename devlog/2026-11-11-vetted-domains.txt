Support can move a new domain off the newdomains IPs
2026-11-11

amelu-ip-pools sends every domain created in the last 7 days from the
newdomains IPs, so a fresh spam account can only hurt those. A legitimate
new customer (hubertstudio.work) asked why their mail scored as spam; the
score came from the domain's age and its .work TLD, plus UCEPROTECT Level 3,
which lists all of OVH (none of our IPs is on Level 1). To give such a
customer the established IPs right away, the script now reads
/etc/amelu-ip-pools/vetted.txt (one domain per line, # for comments) and
leaves those domains out of the newdomains rule. The file only exists on
marduk; hubertstudio.work is the first entry. Backup of the previous script:
/root/amelu-ip-pools.backup-2026-11-11.
