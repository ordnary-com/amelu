First-time tour after onboarding
2026-11-18

New owners of an organization now get a short tooltip tour of the 2.0
dashboard right after the welcome flow (finished or skipped). Onboarding
queues it (TourState.queue in onboarding.ts); it starts once they reach the
Overview, so it never interrupts "Add domain". Each step opens its page and
attaches a tooltip with an arrow to an element marked data-tour="..." (a
ring around it, no dimmed page). If the organization already has a domain
the tour goes into it: the DNS card on the domain page, then Mailboxes,
Address Aliases, DNS Configuration and Spam Filtering in the domain menu
(data-tour="menu:<label>" in shell.html). Without a domain it points at the
"Add a domain" card instead. Then Organization, Billing and the account
menu. Steps whose element isn't on screen (closed drawer on small screens)
are skipped. Esc closes, arrow keys step. Done is remembered per browser and
customer in localStorage; "Take the tour" in the account menu restarts it.
Code in beta/src/app/tour/.
