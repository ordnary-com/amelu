Sidebar: only one category open at a time
2026-11-18

In the 2.0 dashboard's main menu (beta/src/app/shell), unfolding Domains
folds My Organization and the other way round; being on an /organization/
page keeps My Organization open and Domains folded. Older stored state with
both open falls back to Domains. Each category and its pages now share one
list item (groupedCategory, groupedSubitem in shell.ts). A light container
behind unfolded categories was tried and removed again the same night.
