Webmail logo upload is a drop zone with previews
2026-11-18

The logo field on the 2.0 dashboard's /organization/webmail page was a
small box with Upload/Replace and Remove buttons. It is now a drop zone:
drag a file onto it or "choose a file", and it shows the logo side by side
on a white and a dark background, so you can see whether it works on both.
Type (PNG, JPEG, WebP) and size (256 KB, the API's limit) are checked in the
browser first, with a clear message instead of a failed upload. Remove sits
next to it. New "image" icon in icon.ts. API unchanged
(PUT/DELETE /api/organization/webmail/logo).
