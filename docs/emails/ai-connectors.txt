Subject: Supercharge your mail with AI

Hello,

Your inbox has 214 unread emails and a dream. Now your AI assistant can read them, summarize them and draft the replies while you pretend you were just about to get to it. It never gets your password, and your mail stays right where it is.

YOU DECIDE WHAT IT SEES
One mailbox, a whole domain (including mailboxes you add later) or everything in your organization. Whatever you don't switch on stays private, including that thread you'd rather nobody summarized.

IT DRAFTS. YOU HIT SEND.
Reading and drafting are on by default. Drafts wait in your Drafts folder, so nothing goes out that you haven't seen. Sending is off unless you switch it on, and even then it's capped at 20 messages an hour, so it can't answer the whole internet before breakfast.

REGRET-PROOF
Every connection is listed under My Account, Connected Apps. Changed your mind? Disconnect it and it loses access immediately.

PLUG IT IN
In an AI assistant that supports MCP connectors, add a custom connector with the address https://api.amelu.org/mcp and choose OAuth. You'll pop over to Amelu to approve it, then land back in your assistant. Custom connectors depend on your assistant's plan, and only organization owners and admins can connect a mailbox.

Connect your assistant: https://app.amelu.org/account/connected-apps
