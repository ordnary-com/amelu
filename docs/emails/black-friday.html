How to claim it

1. Sign in at app.amelu.org.

2. Open Billing, then Plans, and choose Go or Pro, yearly or monthly.

3. Enter the code at checkout. The price drops before you pay.

Codes are for new Go and Pro subscriptions and only work during their own days (Amsterdam time). One code per organization, and codes can't be combined. After the discounted period your plan renews at the regular price, and you can cancel any time.
