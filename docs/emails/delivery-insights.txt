Subject: See exactly how your mail is delivered

Hello,

Every domain's Overview now shows what happened to the mail it sent in the last 14 days, so you can spot a problem before your recipients tell you about it.

WHAT THE COLOURS MEAN
- Delivered: accepted by the receiving mail server.
- Deferred: temporarily refused. Amelu keeps retrying automatically.
- Rejected: refused for good. The sender got a bounce message.

WHY MAIL WAS REJECTED
Under the chart you'll find the reasons receiving servers gave over the last 7 days, with a plain explanation of each. A common cause is an address that doesn't exist; it's worth removing those from your lists.
