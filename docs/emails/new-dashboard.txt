Subject: The new Amelu dashboard is here

Hello,

app.amelu.org has a new dashboard. We rebuilt it page by page, so the things you do every day take fewer clicks and everything else is easier to find.

EVERY DOMAIN AT A GLANCE
Each domain has an Overview with its mailboxes and aliases, DNS checks, sending against your plan and recent activity, all on one page.

SEE HOW YOUR MAIL IS DELIVERED
A new chart shows what happened to the mail each domain sent in the last 14 days, and why receivers turned any of it down.

HELP WITHOUT LEAVING THE DASHBOARD
Open a ticket, follow the replies and attach screenshots, right where you manage your mail.

ONE ACCOUNT FOR EVERYTHING
You sign in with your Ordnary account, so there's no separate Amelu password to keep track of.

Open the dashboard: https://app.amelu.org
