Subject: Your mail now leaves from Ordnary's own network

Hello,

Since 18 November, every message Amelu sends goes out from Ordnary's own block of IP addresses, 89.106.70.0/24. The addresses inside a large hosting network that we used before are retired.

WHAT THIS CHANGES
Until now, a block aimed at our old hosting provider's network could hit Amelu too, even when our own mail was clean. That's what happened with Outlook and Hotmail addresses. On our own network, only Amelu's mail counts toward our reputation.

New addresses have to build trust with mail providers step by step. Delivery to Outlook and Hotmail addresses improves as they do, which takes some time.

ONE THING TO CHECK
Mail from the new addresses only passes SPF if your domain's SPF (TXT) record includes Amelu. It should read:

v=spf1 include:_spf.amelu.org -all

Your domain's Overview in the dashboard shows whether its SPF record checks out.

Check your domains: https://app.amelu.org/domains

Thank you for your patience while we made this move.
