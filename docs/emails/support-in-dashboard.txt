Subject: Get help without leaving the dashboard

Hello,

Support now lives in the Amelu dashboard. Open a ticket from the Support page and follow the whole conversation there, instead of searching your inbox for replies.

SHOW US WHAT YOU SEE
Attach screenshots, logs or a bounce message to any message: up to five files, 10 MB each and 20 MB in total. Files are always offered as a download, so they can't run anything in your browser.

KNOW WHO YOU'RE TALKING TO
Every reply shows the name of the person on our team who wrote it.

A FASTER ANSWER
While you write a ticket, the Support page shows what to include, such as the address involved and the full error, so we can help on the first reply.

Go to Support: https://app.amelu.org/support

The Amelu team
