Delete {{ ctx.domain()?.name ?? 'this domain' }}

Deleting a domain erases it, and every mailbox, message, alias and setting under it, from the mail server for good. Deactivating only pauses mail and keeps all of that.

What gets deleted

  • Every mailbox and the messages in it.
  • Aliases and settings under the domain.
  • This can't be undone, and the data can't be recovered.

Use this when a domain's data has to go, for example to handle an erasure request under the GDPR, or because the domain is no longer in use. You'll type the domain name to confirm.

Only need a pause?

Deactivate

Deactivating stops mail without losing anything. You can turn it back on at any time.

Moving it elsewhere?

Transfer ownership

A transfer hands the domain and its mail to another Amelu account. Leaving Amelu? Export the mail you want to keep with your mail client first.

Who can delete

Only the organization owner can delete a domain. The deletion is recorded in the organization's activity log.
