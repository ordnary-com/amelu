New owner

The email address they sign in to Amelu with. They need an account already.

What a transfer does

  • Mailboxes, messages, aliases and settings move with the domain.
  • Mail keeps flowing; only who manages it changes.
  • You lose access right away and can't undo it yourself.

You'll be asked to confirm before anything moves.

Before you transfer

  • Only the organization owner can transfer a domain.
  • The new owner needs an existing Amelu account.
  • You can't undo it yourself; the new owner would have to transfer it back.

Just want mail to stop?

Deactivate

Deactivating the domain pauses delivery and keeps everything in your account.
